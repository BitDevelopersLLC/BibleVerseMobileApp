How it works

Set it up once. A new verse is waiting every morning, or every hour, right where you want it.

Android

  1. Open Without Price and pick a translation, a background, where the verse sits on your screen, and how often it changes: every day or every hour.
  2. Turn on "Put a new verse on my lock screen every day."
  3. That's it. A new verse appears on your lock screen each morning (or each hour). Your home screen stays the same.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Add the small Without Price widget to your lock screen. It changes by itself.
  2. Or set a full-screen verse wallpaper, and let Apple's Shortcuts app put a new one on your lock screen every morning. You choose where the verse sits.
  3. The app walks you through both, step by step.
