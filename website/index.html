How it works

Set it up once. A new verse is waiting every morning.

Android

  1. Open Without Price and pick a translation and a background.
  2. Turn on "Put a new verse on my lock screen every day."
  3. That's it. Each morning, today's verse appears on your lock screen. Your home screen stays the same.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Add the Without Price widget under your lock screen clock. It changes by itself every day.
  2. Or save a full-screen verse wallpaper to Photos and set it in a few taps.
  3. The app walks you through both, step by step.
