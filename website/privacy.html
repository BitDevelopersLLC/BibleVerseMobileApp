Privacy Policy

Without Price collects no data. None.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Without Price has:

  • no account or sign-in,
  • no analytics, tracking, or advertising,
  • no crash reporting,
  • no server. The app never connects to the internet.

All Bible verses are built into the app. Your choices (translation, background color, and whether the daily lock screen is on) are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Android permissions

PermissionWhy
SET_WALLPAPERTo put the verse on your lock screen.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDTo keep the daily update running after your phone restarts.
WAKE_LOCKTo let the daily update finish while the screen is off.
FOREGROUND_SERVICERequired by Android's background-job library. Not used to show anything.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATERequired by Android's background-job library. It can only tell whether a network exists. It cannot send or receive data.

The last four come from Android's WorkManager library, which runs the once-a-day update. The app does not have the INTERNET permission, so it is technically unable to send data anywhere.

iPhone and iPad permissions

PermissionWhy
Add to PhotosOnly when you tap "Save today's wallpaper to Photos." The app can add that one picture. It cannot see your photos.

The lock screen widget reads your translation and style choice from storage shared only between the app and its own widget on your device.

This website

withoutprice.app uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers, and loads nothing from other websites.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here and in the app's source code repository.
